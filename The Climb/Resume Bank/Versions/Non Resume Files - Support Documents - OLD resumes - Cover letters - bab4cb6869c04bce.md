Neel Gali
308 N Duncan St, Stillwater, Ok, 74075 | 475-291-1455 | neel.gali@okstate.edu
8/15/2023
,

Dear Shay,
I am Neel Lohit, a graduate CS student at Oklahoma State University, with an expected graduation date in Spring 2024. I am currently entering my third semester this fall.
I am writing to express my strong interest in the opportunity to join your team as an Summer 2024 IT Intern. The prospect of contributing to the seamless operation of your events and ensuring a positive experience for attendees resonates with my passion for technology and teamwork.
What draws me to NE Gas is not only your reputation as a leader in the industry but also your emphasis on integrity, work ethic, character, and diversity. I strongly resonate with these values and believe they align perfectly with my personal and professional principles. Additionally, your commitment to providing challenging and exciting project assignments to interns is particularly appealing to me. As a student with a passion for information technology and a commitment to learning and growth, I am excited about the prospect of contributing to your esteemed organization.
Thank you for considering my application. I am excited about the possibility of becoming a part of your team and contributing to the success of your events. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my skills and enthusiasm align with your needs.
Sincerely,
Neel Gali
