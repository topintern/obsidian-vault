## Professional Experience

**Software Engineer**  |  *Jul 2025 - Present*

*Epik Solutions | Enterprise Platform*

- **Led React.js** and **Typescript frontend development** for a critical **clinical dashboard**. Visualized complex **health data**, ensuring robust **Production Readiness**, **reducing user reporting time** by **25%**.

- **Implemented intelligent health record parsing service**, leveraging **Python** and **OpenAI API** on **AWS EC2**. Enhanced **structured data extraction accuracy** from unstructured notes by **35%**, improving **data quality** significantly.

- **Integrated Claude Code** and **Cursor** into **development workflows** for **full stack applications**. This **accelerated feature delivery**, **improved code quality**, and **decreased debugging time** by **20%** effectively.

- **Designed** and **maintained Microservices Architecture** for **patient engagement platform**. Utilized **Node.js** and **AWS SQS** to ensure high **Reliability**, efficiently **handling 1M daily requests**.

- **Championed cross-functional collaboration** with **clinicians** and product managers, aligning **Typescript frontend functionality** with complex **workflow requirements**. Achieved **95% stakeholder satisfaction** by ensuring precise **product functionality expectations**.

- **Developed RESTful APIs** using **Express.js** for **secure data exchange**. Adhered to **Health IT Interoperability standards** for **provider systems**, **improving data latency** by **15%**.

**Software Engineer**  |  *Jul 2022 - Jun 2025*

*Samay Systems LLC | B2B Platform*

- **Pioneered Full Stack Development** using **React.js** and **Node.js** on **AWS EC2**, enhancing **enterprise client experience** and **boosting platform stability** by **30%**.

- **Optimized Postgresql database queries** for key reporting modules, **improving data retrieval performance** by **60%**. Ensured **system Reliability** for **500+ users**, enhancing responsiveness greatly.

- **Resolved technical ambiguities** in a dynamic **startup environment**, proposing **innovative solutions**. Adapted **System Design** using **AWS** and **Postgresql** to meet **evolving business needs** rapidly and effectively.

- **Implemented backend** utilizing **Microservices Architecture** with **Python** and **AWS Lambda**. Handled **complex business logic**, ensuring **Scalability** for applications, **processing 10M monthly requests** efficiently.

- **Designed** and **maintained Typescript frontend components**, ensuring high **reusability** and consistency across various platform features. Improved **Production Readiness**, **speeding up UI development** by **20%**.

- **Managed CI/CD pipelines** using **Docker** and **AWS CodePipeline**, **automating deployments**. Enabled frequent releases for **Scalability** in **enterprise SaaS applications**, **reducing deployment time** by **50%**.

- **Explored integration** of **AI Coding Tools** for **unit test generation** and **code refactoring**. Utilized internal prompts for **Typescript modules**, effectively **increasing test coverage** by **15%** and efficiency.

**Software Engineer**  |  *Jul 2020 - Aug 2022*

*Zemoso Technologies | SaaS Platform*

- **Contributed to development** of **React.js** and **Typescript web applications**. **Implemented new features**, enhancing existing functionalities for diverse clients, **improving user engagement** by **15%** consistently.

- **Built** and **maintained Node.js backend services**, interfacing with **MySQL databases**. Supported **customer-facing portals**, **processing 100K daily transactions** without errors, ensuring high **system reliability**.

- **Deployed application components** onto **AWS EC2** instances. **Configured servers**, ensuring initial **system stability** and high **Reliability** for **critical business operations**, minimizing disruptions effectively.

- **Participated** in the entire **software development lifecycle**, from gathering requirements to testing **full stack applications**. **Resolved 50+ bugs**, contributing to **timely project delivery** and quality.

- **Designed database schemas** and **managed data migrations** for **Postgresql** and **MySQL systems**. Supported **new feature development**, ensuring **robust data integrity** across applications consistently.

- **Implemented RESTful APIs** for **client-server communication**, handling **authentication** and **data validation**. Ensured **API Security** and **efficient data exchange**, **reducing integration efforts** by **10%**.
