## Summary





- Senior Software Engineer with 5+ years of experience building product-driven, customer-facing systems that power personalization, experimentation, and growth.

- Strong background in full-stack development, microservices, data-informed product iteration, and recommendation-adjacent systems, with proven impact on engagement, conversion, and customer lifetime value.

- Experienced working cross-functionally with Product, Design, Marketing, and Data Science to deliver measurable business outcomes.




## Skills


**Languages & Frameworks**: Ruby on Rails, Kotlin, Java, python, React, TypeScript

**Backend**: Rails, spring boot, Express.js, Microservices architecture, REST APIs, pricing & offer orchestration

**Frontend**: React, Angular, Logged-in dashboards, personalized UI experiences,

**Growth Engineering**: Experimentation (A/B testing), personalization, optimization funnels

**Data & Analytics**: MySQL, MongoDB, Oracle, Cassandra, pyspark,  Event instrumentation, behavioral tracking, feature flags

**Infrastructure**: Cloud-native services (AWS, Azure), CI/CD, Git, monitoring & alerting

**Collaboration**: Product-led development, cross-functional delivery, iteration cycles




## Professional Experience



**Epik Solutions**  |  *July 2025 - Present*

**Senior Software Engineer (Growth Platforms & Personalization)**




- Designed and implemented personalized offer delivery services that surfaced context-aware product recommendations to logged-in users, increasing engagement and repeat interactions.


- Built microservices-based APIs to orchestrate pricing logic, eligibility rules, and offer sequencing, enabling rapid experimentation without frontend rewrites.


- Enhanced the user dashboard experience by integrating personalized data signals and dynamic content, improving clarity and conversion for cross-sell opportunities.


- Partnered closely with Product Managers, Designers, and Marketing to translate growth hypotheses into shippable features backed by measurable KPIs.


- Instrumented event-level analytics and experimentation hooks to support A/B testing, cohort analysis, and iterative optimization.


- Incrementally improved system quality through disciplined pull requests, code reviews, and performance tuning.



**Samay Systems LLC**  |  *Feb 2025 - Jun 2025*

**Senior Software Engineer (Product & Experimentation Systems)**




- Built feature-flagged microservices supporting experimentation and gradual rollout of personalized product experiences.

- Implemented backend logic for targeted recommendations and eligibility filtering, allowing business teams to adjust strategies without redeployments.

- Developed APIs that aggregated behavioral signals and account data to drive personalized content and offers.

- Collaborated with Research and Analytics teams to ensure data collection supported experimentation and conversion analysis.

- Maintained high engineering standards through testing, documentation, and structured technical reviews.



**Arvam Tech**  |  *Jul 2023 - Jan 2025*

**Software Engineer (Full-Stack Product Development)**




- Developed customer-facing dashboards that presented actionable insights and recommendations tailored to user context.


- Built and maintained backend services supporting product workflows, user state, and real-time updates.


- Implemented event tracking and instrumentation to measure feature adoption, engagement, and funnel performance.


- Worked cross-functionally with product and design to refine UX flows based on usage data and feedback.


- Supported continuous delivery through CI/CD pipelines and proactive production monitoring.



**RHYM Tech**  |  *Aug 2022 - May 2023*

**Software Engineer (Product Platforms)**




- Built backend services supporting user engagement workflows and internal tooling.


- Implemented data collection mechanisms to support analytics and future personalization efforts.


- Assisted in debugging production issues and improving system reliability.



**ZeMoSo Technologies**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

**Associate Software Engineer**




- Developed backend and frontend components for web-based products.


- Implemented REST APIs and UI features aligned with evolving product requirements.


- Gained hands-on experience in full-stack development and collaborative delivery.




## Projects



**Personalized Offer Delivery Platform**

Built services to dynamically surface targeted offers based on user behavior, eligibility, and pricing logic.


Enabled rapid experimentation and iteration, directly impacting engagement and customer lifetime value.

**Experimentation-Driven Dashboard Enhancements**
