## Professional Experience

Software Engineer AI | Jun 2025 - Present | Epik Solutions | Enterprise Security AI / Incident Triage

- Built a **React/TypeScript incident-review interface** backed by Python APIs, PostgreSQL, and Redis, consolidating security alerts, evidence, approvals, and escalation into a single operator workflow.

- Translated AI triage output into **prioritized summaries, source citations, confidence scores, and recommended actions**, giving analysts contextual evidence to review before approving remediation.

- Profiled frontend bottlenecks with **Chrome DevTools and Vite**, reducing initial bundle size by **30%** and improving loading performance for real-time enterprise dashboards.

- Integrated **REST APIs and WebSockets** with retrieval services to surface live security events and AI-generated context without fragmenting the analyst's review process.

- Implemented **role-based access, human approval queues, and audit history** across sensitive workflows, connecting user actions to traceable authorization and review decisions.

- Instrumented queue depth, AI latency, failures, and operator decisions to identify workflow bottlenecks, contributing to a **35% reduction in repeat triage cycles**.

Founding Engineer (Full Stack AI) | Jul 2024 - Jun 2025 | Samay Systems LLC | B2B SaaS Compliance AI Platform

- Architected the **React/TypeScript frontend** of a multi-tenant compliance SaaS platform for **15+ enterprise clients**, connecting policy, evidence, and audit workflows to backend APIs.

- Delivered customer-facing features from **discovery and design tradeoffs through implementation, demos, and production iteration**, translating compliance requirements into usable review workflows.

- Integrated **agentic evidence review and RAG** with AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and a compliance knowledge graph, cutting manual review effort by **70%** while preserving traceable sources.

- Authored architecture decisions and design documentation, then used **usage analytics and customer feedback** to refine product experiences and improve user engagement by **20%**.

- Implemented **tenant isolation, RBAC, audit logging, and CI/CD release gates**, supporting reliable delivery of customer-facing changes across the shared SaaS platform.

ML / Software Engineering Intern | Jun 2023 - Aug 2023 | Monarch Tractor | Autonomous Systems & Workflow Automation

- Developed **Vue/TypeScript components with WCAG 2.1 and ARIA practices** for internal provisioning workflows, contributing to an **80% reduction in provisioning time**.

- Integrated **REST APIs and UI improvements** into ML data-labeling infrastructure, increasing annotation throughput by **20%** for downstream training workflows.

Software Engineer | Jul 2020 - Aug 2022 | ZeMoSo Technologies | LabOps / Regulated Workflow SaaS

- Delivered **React/TypeScript LabOps features** backed by Rails, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL for inventory, sample lineage, scheduling, and experiment tracking used by **500+ scientists**.

- Implemented **state management across 10+ modules**, maintaining consistent sample and experiment status through complex, multi-step scientific workflows.

- Built **operational dashboards** for sample queues, upload failures, inventory exceptions, and experiment blockers, giving lab users visibility into issues and reducing support handoffs.

- Connected **role-based interfaces to REST APIs, validation checks, and audit logs**, supporting reliable data capture and approval flows in regulated laboratory operations.

- Added **upload validation, background retries, and failure-state reporting**, making sample-data workflows easier to diagnose and recover when processing failed.

- Authored **100+ automated tests** and partnered with QA and product teams on workflow validation, production fixes, and deployment documentation for regulated releases.
