Dear Hiring Manager,

I am Excited to apply for the Backend Engineer position at Nium. I love the work that Nium does building the real-time payment infrastructure, enabling businesses to collect, convert, and disburse funds globally instantly. I have always wanted to be a part of fintech firms,  and Nium is a great organization for me. I feel my experience working on backend systems aligns with the role. I believe I could be a great addition to your team.
