## Readable content

Dear Hiring Manager,
I am excited to apply for the Junior Developer, Sustaining Team Engagement Platforms role in Adelphi, MD. With over two years of experience designing Power Platform applications, workflows, and BI dashboards, I bring a strong mix of technical expertise and collaborative problem-solving skills that align directly with your team’s mission.
In my current role at EPIK Solutions, I develop and optimize Power Apps and automated workflows using Power Automate, as well as interactive reports and dashboards in Power BI that integrate data from SQL Server and SharePoint. I have also enhanced functionality by integrating REST APIs and third-party services, helping improve automation and engagement platform reliability.
Previously, while working with the OSU IT Department, I collaborated with faculty and staff to design C#/.NET applications and BI dashboards that streamlined institutional reporting. Working in higher education gave me an appreciation for how thoughtfully designed platforms can improve engagement and student success—values that resonate strongly with UMGC’s vision.
I am particularly drawn to this opportunity because of UMGC’s reputation for innovation and its collaborative, student-centered approach. I look forward to bringing my skills in Power Platform development, data integration, and agile collaboration to help sustain and grow engagement platforms that connect UMGC’s students, staff, and faculty.
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background can contribute to your team’s success. Thank you for considering my application, and I look forward to the possibility of speaking with you.
Sincerely,
Lohit Gali
