## Professional Experience

Software Engineer AI | Jun 2025 - Present | Epik Solutions | Enterprise Security / Incident Triage

- Developed **Python REST services and React/TypeScript interfaces** for incident review, approvals, and escalation, integrating PostgreSQL, Redis, and AWS into an end-to-end operational workflow.

- Implemented an **AI-assisted triage pipeline** that organized alerts, logs, and tickets into prioritized summaries with source evidence, helping operators investigate issues and identify next actions.

- Added **telemetry for queue depth, latency, failures, and operator decisions**, exposing recurring workflow bottlenecks and contributing to a **35% reduction in repeat triage cycles**.

- Built **regression evaluations across incident categories** to check duplicate detection, severity reasoning, and remediation guidance as prompts and service behavior changed.

- Integrated **RBAC, OAuth2/OIDC, SSO/SAML, and audit logs** to protect sensitive workflow data and preserve traceability of user actions across enterprise services.

Founding Engineer (Full Stack AI) | Jul 2024 - Jun 2025 | Samay Systems LLC | B2B SaaS Compliance AI Platform

- Implemented **Python/TypeScript services and GraphQL APIs** for a multi-tenant compliance platform serving **15+ enterprise clients**, using Lambda, ECS, SQS, PostgreSQL, and Neo4j.

- Translated **customer requirements into workflows and API/data models**, delivering features through implementation, demos, production fixes, and post-launch measurement.

- Modeled policies, evidence, cloud controls, tickets, and documents as a **structured compliance graph**, enabling source-linked answers and traceable audit records.

- Automated evidence review with retrieval and tool-calling workflows, reducing manual review effort by **70%** while retaining supporting sources for user validation.

- Implemented **CI/CD gates, environment promotion, tenant isolation, and access controls**, supporting repeatable releases and reliable behavior across customer environments.

- Authored **architecture decisions and design documentation**, and added analytics for job latency, queue failures, and evidence gaps to guide improvements and release-readiness reviews.

ML / Software Engineering Intern | Jun 2023 - Aug 2023 | Monarch Tractor | Autonomous Systems / Workflow Automation

- Built **Python telemetry pipelines** that transformed sensor, image, and field-operations data into validated datasets for labeling, analysis, and downstream ML workflows.

- Automated **identity, device, and data-quality checks** across internal operational systems, reducing provisioning and validation time by **80%**.

Software Engineer | Jul 2020 - Aug 2022 | ZeMoSo Technologies | LabOps / Regulated Workflow SaaS

- Developed **Java Spring Boot and Ruby on Rails services** with React/TypeScript interfaces for inventory, sample lineage, scheduling, and experiment tracking used by **500+ scientists**.

- Built **REST APIs, validation checks, and role-based workflows**, maintaining reliable data capture and audit history across regulated scientific review and approval processes.

- Optimized **PostgreSQL queries and background jobs** for high-volume sample events, reducing repeated timeout and debugging cycles in daily laboratory operations.

- Added **input validation, background retries, and failure-state reporting** for uploads and sample-lineage workflows, improving recoverability when processing failed.

- Authored **100+ automated tests** and collaborated with QA and product teams to reproduce defects, validate fixes, and deliver user-facing workflow improvements.

- Documented **deployment behavior, validation notes, and release smoke tests**, helping QA and infrastructure teams verify changes consistently before regulated releases.
