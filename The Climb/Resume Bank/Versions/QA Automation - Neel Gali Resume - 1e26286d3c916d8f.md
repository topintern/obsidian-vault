**Neel Gali**

## Summary

Software engineer with **5 years of experience** across enterprise security, AI compliance SaaS, and LabOps. Combines **Python E2E automation, React/Next.js development, and agentic AI** with Node.js, GraphQL, REST APIs, and containerized delivery. Hands-on with unit testing, TDD, cross-platform testing, and visual regression.

## Professional Experience

**AI Automation Engineer**  |  *2025 - Present*

*Epik Solutions | Enterprise Security*

- Developed **TypeScript/Cypress automation** for multi-step authentication and session-state capture, enabling automated security crawling of complex single-page applications.

- Built **Python and Go microservices** integrating enterprise security platforms, automating 40% of SOC workflows and reducing mean time to resolution by 15 minutes.

- Implemented **AI-driven incident triage** with Python, n8n, and Azure OpenAI, processing 1,000+ daily security events and reducing analyst workload by 30%.

- Delivered **React analytics dashboards** aggregating telemetry from 15+ systems, eliminating 20+ hours of weekly reporting and making operational results accessible to business stakeholders.

- Deployed services with **Docker, Kubernetes, and GitHub Actions** on Azure; resolved silent execution failures in distributed Go runbooks, improving automation success rates by 25%.

**Founding Engineer (Full Stack AI)**  |  *2023 - 2025*

*Samay Systems LLC | AI Compliance SaaS*

- Integrated **Cypress smoke-test suites into GitHub Actions** to continuously validate tenant-isolation boundaries across a multi-tenant compliance platform.

- Delivered **React/TypeScript and AppSync GraphQL** workflows for compliance controls, evidence review, policy authoring, and integration configuration.

- Built **LangChain and AWS Bedrock** compliance automation for evidence collection, summarization, and control scoring, reducing manual auditor effort by 70%.

- Implemented a **LangChain/OpenAI natural-language query interface** through a Flask API, enabling compliance analysts to query Neo4j identity graphs without writing Cypher.

- Orchestrated **Lambda and Step Functions integrations** with Microsoft Entra ID, GitHub, and Jira, modeling identity and activity data in tenant-specific knowledge graphs.

- Designed **tenant isolation** across PostgreSQL, Neo4j, and S3, and provisioned backend infrastructure with AWS CDK, Lambda, ECS, and AppSync.

**Software Engineer**  |  *2020 - 2022*

*ZeMoSo Technologies | LabOps Platform*

- Authored **100+ test cases**, resolved production defects, and integrated **Jenkins quality checks** into delivery pipelines for a regulated scientific platform.

- Built a **TypeScript wrapper for Playwright/Cypress** to validate scientific workflows and capture DOM snapshots for proof-of-concept verification.

- Delivered **React, Spring Boot, and Ruby on Rails** features for a cloud-based robotic lab platform used by 500+ scientists, backed by PostgreSQL microservices.

- Implemented **REST APIs and background jobs** for experiment lifecycle workflows, separating long-running lab data processing from interactive frontend screens.

- Delivered functionality across **10+ product modules**, including inventory, compounds, batch tracking, LCMS uploads, and container linkage, preserving experimental-data traceability.

- Partnered with product and QA teams to translate lab requirements into testable features and coordinate **weekly Agile releases** across a multi-module SaaS platform.
