# Resume tailoring conventions

Standing rules as of 2026-07-16, apply to all newly tailored/generated resume variants going forward.

## Bullet counts per company

- **Epik Solutions**: 6 bullet points
- **Samay Systems LLC**: 7 bullet points
- **Zemoso Technologies**: 6 bullet points
- **No internship entry** (drop Monarch Tractor / any intern role entirely) by default.
  - **Exception**: include the Monarch Tractor internship (1-2 bullets max) when the target role's domain is directly relevant (robotics, autonomous systems, agriculture/industrial tech, sensor/IoT data). Otherwise leave it out.

## Career story per company (fixed domain framing)

Every tailored resume should frame each company's work under this consistent domain story — adjust bullet wording/tech emphasis per JD, but keep the underlying domain framing the same:

- **Epik Solutions** → Enterprise Security
- **Samay Systems LLC** → B2B SaaS Compliance AI
- **Zemoso Technologies** → LabOps / Laboratory-as-a-Service (SaaS)

## Inline bolding convention

Bold **key technologies**, **problems/domains**, and **metrics** inline throughout every bullet — not just tech stack. A reader should be able to scan a single bullet and immediately see what technology was used, what problem/domain it served, and what the quantified impact was.

Example: "Built **Python** and **Go** APIs enabling **compliance controls** for **500+ users**, reducing **mean time by 15 minutes**."

Apply this to all three categories in every bullet where they're present — don't bold only the tech and leave metrics/domain phrases plain.
