## Summary





- Data Engineer with 5+ years of experience building and operating large-scale data systems that power analytics, product insights, and machine-learning workflows

- Deep experience in distributed data processing (Spark), data modeling, and pipeline reliability, with a strong focus on data quality, documentation, and debuggability.

- Comfortable owning ambiguous problem spaces end-to-end and collaborating closely with product, engineering, and data science teams to turn raw data into durable business signals.




## Skills


**Programming**: Python, SQL (advanced), Git

**Distributed Processing**: Spark (batch & near-real-time), large-scale data pipelines

**Data Modeling**: Analytical modeling, event & metric modeling, schema evolution

**Data Platforms**: Data lakes, columnar storage, versioned datasets

**ML Enablement**: Feature pipelines, training datasets, analytics → ML handoff

**Reliability**: Data quality checks, backfills, monitoring, SLA ownership

**Software Practices**: Code reviews, documentation, testing, production debugging

**Collaboration**: Product analytics, growth metrics, experimentation support




## Professional Experience



**CIFC Asset Management**  |  *Jun 2025 - Present*

**Senior Automation Engineer (Enterprise Security & Reporting)**

*Project: CIFC Enterprise Automation & KPI Reporting Modernization*



- Designed and owned distributed Spark pipelines to process multi-domain datasets into analytics-ready fact and dimension models used for executive and operational decision-making.


- Built batch data pipelines that transformed high-volume raw events into curated business metrics, emphasizing correctness, reproducibility, and debuggability.


- Modeled datasets to support trend analysis, cohorting, and time-series analytics, enabling downstream insights without repeated re-processing.


- Implemented data quality validation, anomaly detection, and backfill strategies, improving trust and reducing incident resolution time.


- Partnered with product and business stakeholders to translate ambiguous questions into durable data abstractions rather than one-off queries.


- Authored clear pipeline documentation and data contracts to support long-term maintainability and onboarding.

 



**Samay Systems LLC**  |  *Feb 2025 - Jun 2025*

**Senior Automation Engineer (Cloud & Compliance)**

*Project: Compliance Platform (SaaS) – Security & Compliance Automation*



- Built end-to-end data pipelines that transformed raw operational and application data into feature-ready datasets for analytics and ML workflows.


- Designed schemas optimized for both exploratory analytics and model training, balancing flexibility with performance.


- Developed incremental ingestion pipelines from multiple upstream systems, ensuring idempotency and correctness at scale.


- Collaborated with data scientists to define clear boundaries between data engineering and modeling, accelerating experimentation cycles.


- Owned production reliability by adding monitoring, logging, and failure isolation across pipelines.



**Arvam Tech**  |  *Jul 2023 - Jan 2025*

**Enterprise Automation Engineer**

*Project: Enterprise Asset Management System*



- Implemented Spark-based data transformations to consolidate large, heterogeneous datasets into a unified analytics layer.


- Designed analytical models that supported cross-domain reporting without tightly coupling pipelines to presentation logic.


- Tuned distributed workloads by optimizing joins, partitioning, and file layouts to improve performance at scale.


- Supported downstream analytics teams by ensuring datasets were well-documented, discoverable, and stable over time.






**RHYM Tech**  |  *Aug 2022 - May 2023*

**Associate solutions Engineer**

*Project: Cybersecurity Threat Intelligence Platform*



- Built data pipelines to ingest and normalize high-volume event and telemetry data.


- Supported early-stage analytics and ML initiatives by producing clean, consistent datasets with clear semantics.


- Assisted in debugging production data issues by tracing lineage and validating upstream assumptions.




**ZeMoSo Technologies**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

**Associate Developer**

*Project: Biotech LabOps Automation*



- Developed data ingestion and transformation logic supporting analytical and operational use cases.


- Assisted in schema design and data modeling decisions as product requirements evolved.


- Learned production data engineering fundamentals including testing, documentation, and operational support.




## Projects



**Enterprise Analytics Platform – CIFC**

**Problem**: Business and operational data lived in siloed systems, limiting insight velocity.

**Solution**: Built distributed Spark pipelines and analytical models that enabled reliable, reusable metrics across teams.

**ML-Ready Data Pipelines – Samay Systems**

**Problem**: Data scientists lacked stable, well-modeled datasets for experimentation.

**Solution**: Designed analytics-to-ML data pipelines that produced reproducible, feature-ready datasets.

**Enterprise Asset Management Automation – Arvam Tech**

**Problem**: Manual asset tracking was inefficient.

**Solution**: Automated data flows with PowerShell + Express.js, integrated Power BI, and used Power Automate for approval chains.
