**Software Engineer**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

*Zemoso Technologies | Data Infrastructure & Web Mapping*

- Developed and maintained **Python** scripts for efficient data extraction and transformation (**ETL**) from various sources, processing 500GB of raw data monthly with 98% accuracy.

- Contributed to the design and management of relational **databases** like **MySQL**, ensuring data integrity and optimizing query performance by 20% for reporting.

- Utilized **Microsoft Excel** for preliminary **data analysis** and reporting, preparing summarized datasets for further processing by 20 stakeholders.
