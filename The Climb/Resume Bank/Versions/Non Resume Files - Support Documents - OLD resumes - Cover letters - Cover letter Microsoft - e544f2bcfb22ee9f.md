## Readable content

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my enthusiasm for the Software Development Engineer II position at 343 Industries. As a long-time fan of the Halo franchise, I am thrilled at the opportunity to contribute to a series that has been a significant part of my gaming life. Joining 343 Industries, known for its pioneering work in the sci-fi gaming universe, would be a dream come true.
I hold a master’s degree in computer science from Oklahoma State University and have over two years of technical engineering experience. My background includes a strong focus on game development, cloud solutions, and DevOps practices, all of which align perfectly with the requirements of this role.
During my coursework in video game development, I mastered key concepts using Unity 3D and C#. This included learning the Unity interface, scripting in C#, and working with game objects and components. I implemented Unity's physics engine, integrated animations and audio effects, designed UI elements, and applied various lighting and rendering techniques. I also practiced debugging and optimization strategies and managed assets efficiently. These skills culminated in the development of a comprehensive 3D space shooter game, where I designed intuitive player controls, programmed enemy AI, added power-ups and scoring mechanisms, created engaging levels, and enhanced the game with visual and audio effects.
In my role as a Graduate Research Assistant at Oklahoma State University, I developed applications for an extended reality healthcare training system using Unity, creating immersive and interactive scenarios. Additionally, I managed infrastructure as code (IaC) using Terraform in Azure, ensuring optimized resource allocation and deployment. My experience with Terraform, Azure, and CI/CD pipelines directly reflects your needs for enhancing game developer experience and delivery systems.
At Monarch Tractor, I monitored and optimized CI/CD tools and pipelines, ensuring efficient and reliable software delivery. I also migrated microservices to Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), enhancing application scalability and reliability. These experiences have equipped me with a solid foundation in modern DevOps practices, including containerization, orchestration, and continuous deployment.
As a Full Stack Developer at ZeMoSo Technologies, I created UI components, refactored microservices, and built test cases for a scientific experiment tracking platform. I worked closely with cross-functional teams, improved user interface elements, and resolved critical issues, demonstrating my ability to collaborate effectively and deliver high-quality software solutions.
The Halo series is more than just a game to me; it is a source of inspiration and creativity. The opportunity to work with 343 Industries and collaborate with talented teams to build the next generation of games and experiences in the Halo universe is incredibly exciting. I am eager to bring my background in game development, cloud solutions, and CI/CD pipeline optimization to your team, ensuring the successful and on-time delivery of the next generation of Halo products.
Warm regards,
Neel Gali
