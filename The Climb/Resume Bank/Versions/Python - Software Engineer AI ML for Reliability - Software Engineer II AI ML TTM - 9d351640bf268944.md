## Professional Experience



**Epic Solutions**  |  *Jul 2025 - Present*

**Software Engineer II**

*Cloud Operations Platform (Enterprise SaaS)*



- Designed and delivered backend services supporting a centralized incident management platform, enabling on-call engineers to triage, mitigate, and resolve service incidents efficiently across multiple cloud workloads.


- Built event-driven ingestion pipelines to collect incidents, alerts, and operational signals from distributed services, normalizing data to support analytics and AI-driven insights.


- Applied AI-assisted workflows (prompt + context engineering) to summarize incident timelines, surface recurring patterns, and recommend mitigation steps, reducing manual cognitive load for responders.


- Implemented KQL-based analytics over large volumes of incident and telemetry data to identify trends, correlate signals, and support proactive reliability improvements.


- Improved service reliability by introducing health probes, retries, circuit breakers, and automated validation, preventing cascading failures during high-severity incidents.


- Actively participated in live-site operations, including on-call rotations, incident response, root cause analysis, and post-incident reviews, driving long-term fixes rather than short-term patches.



**Samay Systems LLC**  |  *July 2024 - Jun 2025*

**Software Engineer I**

*Cloud Reliability & Automation Team*



- Developed cloud-native services in Azure to automate operational workflows, reducing manual incident handling and improving response consistency.


- Built internal tooling to enrich incident data with service metadata, ownership context, and historical signals, enabling faster and more accurate triage.


- Implemented data pipelines and queries using Kusto (KQL) to analyze operational telemetry and support reliability dashboards used by engineering and leadership teams.


- Collaborated across multiple teams (engineering, product, operations) to design solutions aligned with real-world on-call pain points.


- Strengthened engineering quality by writing comprehensive unit, integration, and regression tests, and by participating in structured design and code reviews.

**ZeMoSo Technologies**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

**Associate Developer**

*Biotech LabOps*



- Designed and implemented backend APIs supporting high-volume application workflows, emphasizing correctness, performance, and security.

- Participated in on-call support and post-incident analysis, strengthening skills in debugging, root-cause analysis, and long-term fixes.

- Participated in system debugging and production support, strengthening operational reliability.




## Projects



**AI-Powered Incident Insights Engine**




- **why**: On-call engineers spent excessive time parsing logs and timelines during outages.


- **how**: Built a service to generate AI-assisted summaries and insights for live incidents by combining structured incident data, KQL-derived signals, and prompt-engineered LLM workflows to produce concise, actionable summaries.


**Operational Signal Analytics Platform**




- **why**: Fragmented data limited the ability to detect patterns and prevent recurring incidents.


- **how**: Developed a centralized analytics layer for incidents and service telemetry by ingesting telemetry into Kusto, built optimized KQL queries, and exposed insights through internal dashboards and APIs.



**System Health & Monitoring Framework**




- Built a Python-based monitoring layer to track latency, error rates, and throughput.


- Enabled faster incident diagnosis and improved overall system stability.
