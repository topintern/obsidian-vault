## Summary





- **Automation Engineer** with 5+ years of experience building production-grade **data and evaluation frameworks** across **financial environments**, **compliance platforms**, and large-scale **enterprise systems**,

- Proven ability to translate business and research requirements into scalable, reproducible systems using **Python, SQL, cloud data platforms**, and testing frameworks that enable engineers, analysts, and QA teams to define, run, and trust metrics across large, complex systems.

- Deep expertise in  **Azure Databricks**,  **PySpark, Delta Lake, and ADLS Gen2**, with a strong track record of
building governed, high-reliability  **ETL/ELT pipelines** for batch and streaming workloads.

- Proven ability to translate complex business and scientific workflows into optimized data models, enforce
data governance with Unity Catalog, and deliver  **SLA-driven pipelines through CI/CD** and **monitoring-first design**

- Hands-on experience designing  **LLM-powered agents, AI-assisted analysis workflows**, data processing, and security controls across **AWS, Azure, and enterprise environments**.

- Experienced in building end-to-end applications across **frontend, backend, and cloud infrastructure**, collaborating closely with product managers, UX designers, and partner engineering teams.




## Skills


**Languages & Libraries**: Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch

**Databases & Data Management**: SQL (MySQL, PostgreSQL), NoSQL (MongoDB, DynamoDB)

**Data Engineering**: Databricks, Snowflake, ETL, Data Pipelines, data lakes, medallion architecture (bronze/silver/gold), ETL/ELT pipelines, Apache Kafka

**Cloud Platforms & Services**: AWS (Lambda, S3, EC2, IAM), Azure (ADLS Gen2, Fabric, Logic Apps, AD), GCP (Cloud Functions)

**CI/CD & DevOps Tools**: Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform

**AI & Agentic Systems**: LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic),  Frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face, BedRock ), Prompt Engineering, Tool-Calling Agents, RAG, Vector Search

**Machine Learning**: supervised & unsupervised learning, classification, regression, clustering, model validation

**Analytics & Statistics**: exploratory data analysis, hypothesis testing, feature engineering, statistical modeling

**Scripting & Automation**: PowerShell, Python, Bash, Power Automate, VBScript, VisualCV

**Data Visualization & Reporting**: Power BI, Tableau, Grafana





## Professional Experience



**CIFC Asset Management**  |  *Jul 2025 - Present*

**Senior Automation Engineer (Enterprise Security & Reporting)**

*Project: Financial Services / Asset Reporting Modernization*



- Designed Python-based analytical workflows that translate complex financial questions into repeatable computations, enabling faster hypothesis testing and exploratory research.


- Developed automated KPI and performance dashboards aligned with executive and investment-risk stakeholders, transforming raw datasets into decision-ready insights.


- Implemented data validation, reconciliation, and monitoring frameworks to ensure numerical accuracy and auditability in regulated asset-management workflows, reducing downstream model noise and reporting discrepancies.


- Applied LLM-assisted analysis pipelines to accelerate exploratory data analysis and summarization across large financial and security datasets while preserving deterministic outputs for compliance.


- Designed and automated SSRS reports integrated with Dynamics 365 (D365) to streamline operational reporting, ensuring accuracy and faster delivery of financial and business insights.


- Built research-grade financial data pipelines that ingest, normalize, and version investment, operational, and control datasets to support risk analysis, trend modeling, and portfolio insights, ensuring consistency across research and reporting layers.


- Engineered ML-ready feature datasets to enable applied statistical and machine learning models for anomaly detection, forecasting, and pattern discovery across financial and operational time-series data.







**Samay Systems LLC**  |  *Jul 2024 - Jun 2025*

**Senior Automation Engineer (Cloud & Compliance)**

*Project: Compliance Platform (SaaS) – Security & Compliance Automation*



- Developed Python-based automation services that collect, validate, and summarize evidence from AWS, Azure, GitHub, and Jira via secure APIs.

- Built AI-assisted compliance workflows combining LLM reasoning with structured cloud data to guide customers through audits, investigations, and risk analysis.

- Implemented RAG-style querying over historical compliance data, allowing customers to ask natural-language questions and receive contextual answers.

- Designed and trained supervised and unsupervised ML models addressing compliance and industry-specific analytic use cases.

- Built reusable frontend and backend components to reduce duplication and improve consistency across
features, accelerating delivery while maintaining quality




**Arvam Tech**  |  *Jul 2023 - May 2024*

**Enterprise Automation Engineer**

*Project: Enterprise Asset Management System*



- Engineered data ingestion and transformation pipelines to consolidate asset and operational datasets into analytics-ready formats used for trend analysis and forecasting.

- Built event-driven workflows to process streaming updates and ensure near-real-time availability of analytical data.

- Delivered interactive dashboards and metrics frameworks that surfaced utilization patterns and operational risks for decision-makers.

- Developed n8n workflows to sync asset inventory and process webhook-driven events.

- Integrated Power BI dashboards with gateway refresh for asset tracking and usage metrics.

- Implemented UI enhancements and backend integrations to improve usability and reliability of internal
tools




**RHYM Tech**  |  *Aug 2022 - May 2023*

**Associate solutions Engineer**

*Project: Cybersecurity Threat Intelligence Platform*



- Developed analytical models using Python and SQL to analyze high-volume telemetry and event data.

- Performed data cleaning, transformation, and feature extraction to support downstream ML tasks

- Developed Python-based data pipelines to ingest high-volume event data into structured stores, enabling downstream statistical analysis and pattern detection.

- Applied time-series aggregation and anomaly detection logic to identify emerging trends across security telemetry.

- Created analytical dashboards to visualize distributions, trends, and risk indicators for technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Supported production deployments by validating application behavior, monitoring early usage signals,
and addressing issues discovered after release



**ZeMoSo Technologies**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

**Associate Developer**

*Project: Biotech LabOps Automation*



- Developed modern web application features using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, and React,
ensuring responsive design and cross-browser compatibility.

- Implemented backend APIs and data access layers to support UI-driven workflows, balancing
correctness, performance, and maintainability.

- Supported data science initiatives through data preparation, exploratory analysis, and basic
modeling.

- Assisted in building analytical pipelines and reports aligned with business requirements.




## Projects



**AI-based Security Reporting Platform**

**Problem**: Security teams relied on manual reporting and ad-hoc investigations across fragmented tools.

**Solution**: Built AI-driven automation and self-updating dashboards using LLM agents, APIs, and Fabric to deliver continuous insights at scale.

**Agent-based Compliance Dashboard – RHYM Tech**

**Problem**: Audit preparation and evidence validation were time-consuming and error-prone for customers.

**Solution**: Implemented LLM-assisted compliance workflows and RAG-based querying to automate evidence collection and analysis.

**Enterprise Asset Management Automation – Arvam Tech**

**Problem**: Manual asset tracking was inefficient.

**Solution**: Automated data flows with n8n, azure data factory, ADLS Gen 2, integrated Power BI, and used Power Automate for approval chains.
