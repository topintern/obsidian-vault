ESSAY

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am excited to submit my application for the MBA program at the McCombs School of Business. The Texas McCombs MBA program stands out as the ideal platform to bridge my technical expertise with my career aspirations in finance.

Graduating with a master’s degree in computer science, I have cultivated a strong foundation in technology and its applications in various business contexts. Throughout my academic journey, I have consistently demonstrated an aptitude for problem-solving and understanding of software development principles.

My professional experiences have further refined my technical and analytical skills. As a Full Stack Developer at Oklahoma State University, I enhanced UI responsiveness and optimized database queries, leading to significant improvements in performance metrics. At Monarch Tractor, I designed an employee lifecycle management workflow, reducing manual workload by 50%, empowering a cost-effective solution, saving at least 30% of the budget through contributions during requirement analysis and technical feasibility assessments of vendor proposals and increasing workflow efficiency by 60% through automation and scripting, These roles required me to employ critical thinking and innovative solutions to complex challenges, skills that are directly transferable to the fast-paced environment of investment banking.

Additionally, my tenure at ZeMoSo Technologies in Hyderabad involved creating UI components and refactoring microservices for a scientific experiment tracking platform. I worked closely with cross-functional teams, improving design consistency and integrating new features for user roles and permissions management.

Beyond my technical roles, I have led projects such as the development of a Virtual Incubation Centre, a platform facilitating interactions between entrepreneurs, mentors, and investors. This project, along with creating a blogging platform using Ruby on Rails, has given me a unique perspective on the intersection of technology and business, reinforcing my desire to transition into investment banking.

The Texas McCombs MBA program’s emphasis on experiential learning and its strong industry connections align perfectly with my career objectives. I am particularly excited about the Investment Fund and the opportunity to apply classroom knowledge to real-world investment decisions. The collaborative and innovative culture at McCombs resonates with my values, and I look forward to contributing to and learning from a diverse cohort of peers.

In the long term, I aim to leverage my technical background and the comprehensive business education from McCombs to advance to a senior leadership position in investment banking. Specializing in technology sector deals, I aspire to become a trusted advisor, driving strategic initiatives and contributing to the growth and success of both clients and my firm.

In conclusion, my strong technical foundation, professional experiences, and unwavering commitment to a career in investment banking make me a strong candidate for the Texas McCombs MBA program. I am eager to bring my passion, dedication, and unique perspective to the McCombs community and to take full advantage of the opportunities that the program offers.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to and growing with the Texas McCombs MBA program.

Sincerely,
Neel Gali
