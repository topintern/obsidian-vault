## Profile

**Software Developer** with **4+ years** building **Higher-ed student information systems** for students, faculty, staff, and institutional partners. Strong in **Python**, **Django**, **REST APIs**, **HTML/CSS/JavaScript**, and **PostgreSQL**, **Oracle**, with hands-on experience across **Canvas LMS**, **Banner SIS**, and partner-facing academic workflows.

## Professional Experience

**Software Developer**  |  *Aug 2024 - Present*

*CYAN tech | Higher Education Platform*

- Build **Python**/**Django** student information modules for dual-credit enrollment, roster, grading, and partner reporting workflows, supporting **50,000+ students**, instructors, and district staff.

- Integrate **Canvas LMS**, **Ellucian Banner**, and **PeopleSoft SIS** with **REST APIs** and scheduled sync jobs, processing **200+ course sections per term** and removing **15 hours/week** of manual reconciliation.

- Design **Django REST Framework** services with custom serializers, permission rules, and **Celery**/**Redis** background processing, reducing nightly **SIS** batch loads from **6 hours to 45 minutes**.

- Tune **PostgreSQL**, **Oracle**, and **MySQL** operational data sources using indexes, query rewrites, and materialized views, cutting staff report generation from **10 minutes to under 30 seconds**.

- Modernize legacy **PHP**/**MySQL** grade-reporting tools into maintainable **Django ORM** services, retiring **12,000 lines** of brittle code with **zero partner-facing downtime**.

- Establish **Pytest**, Django test client, and integration-test procedures across **85% of SIS paths**, shipping through **Docker** and **Jenkins CI/CD** while reducing **production defects by 35%**.

- Lead **Git**/**GitHub** pull-request review, branch protection, release tagging, and peer feedback for a **6-developer team**, reducing **merge conflicts by 40%** and review cycles by **15%**.

- Translate ambiguous staff requests into **OpenAPI** contracts, acceptance criteria, runbooks, and weekly **Jira** status updates, managing **4-6 concurrent projects** with clear timeline and risk communication.

**Graduate Research Assistant & Application Developer**  |  *Aug 2023 - Aug 2024*

*Oklahoma State University | Department of Computer Science*

- Delivered **Python**/**Django**/**MySQL** registration and advising modules for **10,000+ student facing** per enrollment cycle, owning requirements analysis, design, programming, testing, and release support.

- Built **REST API integrations**, **LTI**, **OAuth 2.0**, and **SIS feed** integrations between departmental systems, **Banner SIS**, and **Canvas LMS**, automating **15 student-data workflows** and replacing manual CSV handoffs for staff users.

- Developed **HTML**, **CSS**, **JavaScript**, and **Bootstrap** advising dashboards that surfaced at-risk student data with **95% accuracy** for faculty advisors and program coordinators.

- **Debugged complex legacy modules** and rewrote **MySQL** queries, joins, and stored procedures, reducing administrative report runtimes from **10 minutes to under 1 minute**.

- Coordinated **Git**/**GitHub** branching, code reviews, release tags, and project documentation across **3 research applications**, keeping shared code stable for rotating contributors.

- Partnered with campus IT on **Linux**, **Nginx**, **Gunicorn**, and **AWS EC2** deployment, improving application reliability to **99.9%** during registration and advising periods.

**Application Developer**  |  *Aug 2021 - Aug 2022*

*Keshav Memorial Institute of Technology | Campus Web Systems*

- Built responsive **HTML**, **CSS**, **JavaScript**, and **jQuery** interfaces for college web applications, improving **self-service access** and increasing **student engagement by 20%**.

- Developed **Python**/**Flask** and **PHP** application logic over **MySQL**, supporting academic services, faculty workflows, and student-facing tools for **10,000+ users**.

- Automated admissions, attendance, and **academic reporting** with **Python**  and **SQL** stored procedures, replacing spreadsheet-heavy processes and saving staff **8 hours/week**.

- Tested and **debugged modules** before **peak registration periods** using **Python** validation scripts and load checks, reducing **critical defects by 30%** while preserving enrollment uptime.

- Delivered **3 concurrent campus projects** through **Git** feature branches, peer review, and milestone tracking, completing **90% of releases on time** while balancing support requests.
