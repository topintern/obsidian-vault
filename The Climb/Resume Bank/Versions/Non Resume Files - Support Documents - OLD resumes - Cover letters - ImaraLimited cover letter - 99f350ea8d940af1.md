Dear Barry Hardy,
I am writing to express my interest in the Software Support Engineer position at your esteemed company. With a robust background in Full Stack Development and software support, coupled with a proven track record of enhancing performance and user satisfaction, I am excited about the opportunity to contribute to your team and support your global customers.
In my recent role as a Full Stack Developer (Graduate Research Assistant) at Oklahoma State University, I collaborated with a team to enhance a student portal, significantly improving its performance and user experience. My experience in implementing unit testing for front-end and backend components, conducting regular code reviews, and leading user acceptance testing aligns well with the diagnostic and collaborative responsibilities of the Software Support Engineer role.
During my tenure at Monarch Tractor as a Software Development Intern, I spearheaded the development of an automation workflow that reduced manual work by 80%, streamlined data exchange between APIs, and utilized AWS services for efficient script execution and data management. This experience has equipped me with the skills necessary to handle complex software support issues, optimize workflows, and ensure seamless integration and real-time data synchronization.
At ZeMoSo Technologies, I managed a sophisticated platform of AI-controlled robotic labs, contributed to feature additions, and resolved critical issues with non-persisting data. My expertise in DevOps tools, cloud technologies, and various programming languages like Python and C++ will be beneficial in diagnosing Linux/Ubuntu-related issues and improving overall technical support efficiency.
The role at your company excites me because of your industry-leading position in Robotics, Computer Vision, and Control Automation within the Mechatronics domain. I am particularly drawn to the growth-oriented nature of the role and the opportunity to work alongside a highly talented and educated team.
Here are some specific skills and experiences that I bring to the table:
- Basic Linux Operating System Knowledge: Proficient in diagnosing Linux/Ubuntu-related logs, command line/terminal/SSH skills, and software installation for updates.
- Troubleshooting Experience: Skilled in checking log files, restarting processes, and identifying problem components.
- Programming Skills: Proficient in C/C++, Python, and other languages, which is highly desirable for the role.
- Version Control: Experienced with Git and other source control systems.
- Configuration Management: Knowledgeable in config management tools and best practices.
I am confident that my technical background, problem-solving skills, and commitment to customer experience make me a strong fit for this role. I am excited about the opportunity to bring my expertise to your team and contribute to your company's success.
Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how my skills and experiences align with the needs of your team. Please feel free to contact me at (475) 291-1455 or via email at neellohit69@gmail.com to schedule an interview.
Sincerely,
Neel Gali
