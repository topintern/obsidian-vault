*Samay Systems LLC | B2B Healthcare Compliance & Regulatory AI Platform*

- Developed fullstack **React + GraphQL** applications enabling **compliance controls, policy authoring, audit trails**, and document management for **10,000+ users** across regulated healthcare and enterprise workflows.

- Orchestrated nightly identity graph ingestion using **AWS Step Functions** and **Lambda**, modeling enterprise **Entra ID, GitHub, and Jira** data into per-tenant **Neo4j** knowledge graphs automatically.

**Software Engineer**  |  *Jul 2021 - Aug 2022*

*ZeMoSo Technologies | Pharmaceutical LabOps Platform*

- Authored **100+ automated test cases** and resolved critical production issues, integrating **Jenkins** CI/CD pipelines to enforce quality checks and promote artifacts in a regulated platform.

- Integrated third-party instrument **APIs** and **LCMS** data parsers into backend services, enabling automated ingestion of raw experimental results and reducing manual data entry by **35%**.
