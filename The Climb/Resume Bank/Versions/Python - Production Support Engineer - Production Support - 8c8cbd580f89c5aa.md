## Professional Experience


**Senior Production Support Engineer**  |  *Jul 2025 - Present*

*CIFC Asset Management | Investment Platform*




- Monitored **Sidekiq** and **Kafka** job schedules for daily portfolio valuation and trade settlement workflows, triaging stalled jobs and escalating failures with documented root cause analysis.

- Triaged Level 1 trade feed and API failures by parsing structured logs, isolating root causes, and preparing Level 2 escalation packages for the engineering team.

- Wrote **Python** scripts to parse market data feed logs and detect anomalous patterns, alerting on-call engineers and reducing production incident detection time by 40% platform-wide.

- Queried **PostgreSQL** to validate portfolio and trade data correctness during incidents, writing targeted **SQL** to trace bad records and confirm fix effectiveness before closing tickets.

- Provided first-line support for portfolio managers and operations staff via **Microsoft Teams**, triaging investment platform alerts and routing unresolved issues to the development engineering team.

- Authored incident postmortems, triage guides, and runbooks in **Confluence**, documenting root causes, resolution steps, and preventive measures that reduced repeat production incident recurrence by 30%.

- Configured **PagerDuty** alert routing for trade feed failures and investment platform API errors, ensuring on-call engineers received actionable notifications within two minutes of detection consistently.

- Built **Grafana** dashboards tracking job success rates, trade feed latency, and portfolio error rates, giving operations teams real-time **SLA** visibility and cutting interruptions by 50%.



**Production Support Engineer**  |  *Jul 2022 - Jun 2025*

*Samay Systems LLC | B2B Compliance Platform*




- Queried **SQL Server**, **Oracle**, and **MongoDB** to investigate data anomalies and platform issues, developing complex joins and aggregations that reduced data investigation time by 50%.

- Supported web-based compliance APIs built in **Rails**, diagnosing **HTTP** errors, tracing request chains through logs, and delivering root cause analysis to the development engineering team.

- Monitored **Python** **Celery** async task queues on the compliance platform, identifying stalled workers, recovering failed jobs, and documenting queue saturation incidents with root cause findings.

- Debugged **Python** compliance service failures by reading source, tracing stack traces, and isolating logic errors to produce fully documented escalation packages for the development team.

- Wrote **Python** support automation scripts to streamline repetitive compliance data lookups, cutting analyst investigation time by 35% and freeing the team for higher-priority escalation work.

- Monitored **Redis** memory utilization and key eviction rates on the compliance platform, alerting engineering when cache saturation threatened stability and preventing three major service outages.

- Wrote **Python** reconciliation scripts comparing **SQL Server** and **MongoDB** compliance records, detecting 12,000+ discrepant records monthly and producing audit-ready mismatch reports for the engineering team.

- Monitored **Elasticsearch** cluster health on the compliance platform, diagnosing shard failures and mapping conflicts, and coordinating with engineering to restore search availability during production outages.



**Associate Support Engineer**  |  *Jul 2020 - Aug 2022*

*Zemoso Technologies | LabOps Platform*




- Built **Python** scripts to automate log parsing and data validation for the LabOps platform, reducing manual incident investigation time by 45% for the support team.

- Wrote complex **PostgreSQL** queries to trace data lineage across lab sample workflows, identifying corrupt records and supporting root cause analysis for recurring data quality incidents.

- Tracked scheduled **Oracle** batch jobs on the LabOps platform, detecting execution failures, analyzing logs for timeout root causes, and escalating feed failures with documented evidence.

- Documented LabOps platform support procedures and incident runbooks in **Confluence**, enabling junior engineers to resolve recurring incidents independently and reducing overall escalation volume by 25%.

- Triaged failed **GitHub Actions** CI deployments for the LabOps web platform, analyzing build logs, coordinating fixes with engineering, and documenting resolution steps in **Confluence** runbooks.




## Skills

**Monitoring & Observability**: Prometheus, Grafana, PagerDuty, structured log analysis, SLA dashboards, alerting
