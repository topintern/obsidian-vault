## RESUME WRITING AND FORMATTING

- Goal: a strong, credible, interview-generating application. Never promise a callback or invent an ATS score.
- Use achievement-oriented bullets: action + concrete system/problem + technical approach + defensible outcome. No conversational scripts, filler, or generic responsibility lists.
- Prioritize the JD's main work, not every optional keyword. Backend roles should lead with APIs, data, performance, reliability and operations; AI roles should show production workflows, integrations, evaluations and safeguards.
- Use clean, selective bolding: normally 1–2 short spans per bullet emphasizing a key capability/tool and meaningful result. Avoid whole bold sentences or bolding every technology.
- Keep company/title/section hierarchy clear. Skills category labels are bold; skills themselves are mostly normal weight.
- Typical template: XCharter, 10pt body, A4, about 0.5–0.55in side margins. Preserve readability; don't shrink the font to cram in more bullets.
- If I ask for extra bullets, add the requested count in the requested companies. Avoid duplicates and invented metrics; tighten wording before compressing spacing.
- Preserve my manual changes, even if they differ from your earlier draft. Never silently restore a deleted subtitle or LinkedIn row.
- Compile, confirm page count, check text/links, render the PDF and visually inspect for clipping, overlap, awkward wraps and excessive bolding.
- Keep the final response short: finished artifact, the main changes, verification, and any genuinely important caveat.
