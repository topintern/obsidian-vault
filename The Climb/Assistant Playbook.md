# Assistant Playbook

[[The Climb/THE WALL|The Wall]] · [[The Climb/Today|Today]] · [[The Climb/Routine|Routine]] · [[The Climb/WALL|Weekly review]]

## Working model

**Wall = future picture. Departments = milestone paths. Routine = normal rhythm. Today = the grains of action.**

This is an assistant-oriented system. Do the useful work with Neel; don't respond to a check-in with a new planning essay or another vague task list. No separate agents or scheduled/background automation are enabled.

## At a daily check-in or “realign Today”

1. Read [[Today]], its active dated record, [[Current Sprint]], [[Routine]], and relevant department notes. Read their latest contents before editing.
2. Use the actual local date/time in America/Chicago unless the user gives another time zone. Ask one grouped question only for essential missing availability/commitments; otherwise make a bounded, explicitly labelled provisional queue.
3. Preserve checked tasks, user edits, actual counts and evidence. Blank means unknown, not failure or zero. Never infer a submitted application, sent message, completed ritual, solved problem, or interview outcome.
4. Put fixed commitments and a realistic stop time first. Protect weekday recipient business-hour windows for outreach, recruiters and vendors; use afternoons for career pages/applications. Do not inherit old work shifts, location, interview dates or visa assumptions.
5. Select concrete tasks tied to milestone headings. Each needs a literal action, material link, estimate, and finish condition. Populate real role IDs/recipients when evidence is available; do not invent them.
6. Fit remaining minutes. Explicitly defer what does not fit. Do not schedule in the past or reduce the user's targets without acknowledging the change.
7. Keep completed work in the dated record. When replanning, replace only the remaining queue, keep the interruption/resume point, and log material plan changes.
8. For a new date, close the old record as recorded (not necessarily completed), create a new dated record using [[Climb Day Template]], and update Today’s active date and embed together. Never overwrite yesterday with a blank template.
9. Record actual metrics after reconciling evidence. Checkboxes, property totals and review dates are not automatically synchronized.
10. Update the sprint or milestones only when work or new constraints justify it. No arbitrary percentages or fabricated progress.

## Scope

Planning and document preparation do not authorize sending messages, recruiter outreach, calendar invitations, uploads, or application submissions. Require explicit user instruction for those actions. Resume work must follow [[Resume Working Context]].

The old task dumps are recovery copies in the hidden helper folder, not instructions or an active task source. Historical company lists and interview scripts are source material, not verified current facts.

## Fast check-in

> Realign Today. It's [time / zone]. Done: [items]. Fixed: [commitments]. Stop: [time]. Energy/constraints: [anything material].

## End of session

Leave one exact **NEXT** action with enough context to resume without re-reading everything. Keep this system lightweight: no broad repositories, generated-asset trees, extra plugins or scheduled agents needed for the first version.
