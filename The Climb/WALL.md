## Twenty-minute review

1. Read the week's dated records in [[Climb Progress.base]]. Separate blanks from zeroes.
2. Compare actual outputs with each department's next milestone. Move a milestone only when its evidence exists.
3. In Hunt, inspect current roles, unique applications, outreach, replies, interviews and follow-ups. Don't compare immature cohorts as if response windows were complete.
4. In Prep, retrieve an answer or solution cold. Mark the weak areas for review, not just the pages you read.
5. Note spiritual practice, movement, creative outputs and real connection without inventing a single aggregate life score.
6. Adjust next week's priorities and capacity. Carry forward only work deliberately selected again.
