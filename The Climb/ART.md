# Art & Expression

[[The Climb/THE WALL|The Wall]] · [[The Climb/Today|Today]] · [[The Climb/Routine|Routine]] · [[The Climb/WALL|Weekly review]]

> [!abstract] The destination
> Become someone who makes and finishes things: filmmaking, AI video, writing, music, or a small YouTube experiment. One consistent hour can compound—but not if it becomes an hour of choosing tools.

## Starting point

The latest interests are filmmaking/AI video, writing, music and YouTube. Piano and dance remain older candidates, not a presumed current commitment. Pick one small output first; the medium can change after an actual trial.

## The daily hour

Choose **one active artifact**. Today's task names the next making step: draft a 200-word scene, storyboard six shots, edit a 30-second sequence, or rehearse a chosen passage.

These are action examples, not assignments already chosen for you. Avoid a fresh project every day. Online-business/product experiments belong to [[OWN BUILD]] and share this hour when selected; they are not an additional mandatory lane.
