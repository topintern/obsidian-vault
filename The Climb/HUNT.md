## Daily operating targets

- **4–5 hours** normally budgeted across people-work and applications.
- **50–100** is the requested high-volume ambition for qualified application activity centered on active suitable employers. Record **unique employers checked and unique submitted roles separately**; multiple applications to one employer are not multiple companies.
- **20–30 targeted LinkedIn/networking cold DMs** is the requested outreach ambition. Include useful personalization; drafts are not sends.
- Every week, deliberately review the **top 10 employers actually pursued** for engineering fit and credible current hiring. Famous names alone do not qualify a week as successful.
- Role mix includes SWE/full-stack, backend, AI engineering and relevant cloud/platform work. Choose by supported experience, not keyword accumulation.
- If the target does not fit actual capacity or suitable openings, report the gap rather than count weak/duplicate applications or silently lower the target.

## Morning people queue

1. Replies and time-sensitive recruiter/vendor conversations.
2. Warm/referral opportunities. Previous names: Sharan, Ananth, Apple, Google—**status unknown; check actual messages first**.
3. Relevant recruiter and hiring-team follow-ups with a clear next action.
4. Targeted LinkedIn/networking outreach, grouped by employer/role; stop repeating generic messages.

Respect recipient working hours. C2C/Dice/vendor opportunities stay separate from direct-employer opportunities; do not infer work authorization, sponsorship needs or suitability.

## Afternoon application queue

Use career pages and boards for discovery, then verify the actual role. LinkedIn, Jobright, Remote Rocketship, Tsenta and Dice are user-selected channels, not independent quotas or proof of submission.

**Per role:** current opening → fit/eligibility check → select/tailor supported resume → user-authorized submission → record actual confirmation and next step.

Use [[Resume Working Context]] for resume work. A prepared PDF, opened page, saved job or reported automated application is not a verified submission.
