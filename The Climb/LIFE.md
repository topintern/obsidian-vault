## How it fits

Possible interests from the prior plan include dance (West Coast Swing / Telugu / Bollywood-style), a recurring group or comedy. They remain options, not commitments. Research the current local details only when we choose to act.

When an activity is selected, put its real time and travel in Today before packing the remaining work. One weekly plan can be useful; it need not become a daily administrative checklist.
