> [!abstract] The destination
> Stronger, more athletic, visibly healthier in appearance, and training as part of ordinary life rather than another restart project.

## Starting point

Current strength, training frequency, equipment and constraints are not yet recorded. Older pull-up/dip/dumbbell goals are interests, not established ability. Set performance targets only after a real baseline.

## Milestones

| Point | Target picture | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Oct 21** | Record a present baseline and choose the next two feasible sessions | Actual exercises/loads/reps or other chosen measures, equipment and available slots |
| **B · Oct 31** | Select a weekly rhythm that fits the career sprint | Sessions actually completed and time cost; no arbitrary performance promises |
| **C · Nov 30** | Repeat the chosen training pattern and compare like-for-like measures | Training log and one baseline comparison |
| **D · Jan 31** | Visible consistency and progressive improvement in chosen measures | Repeatable observations, not a fabricated fitness score |
| **E · Apr 4** | Stronger, more athletic, sustainable rhythm | Compare actual strength/conditioning and personally chosen appearance measures with baseline |

## How it fits

Movement/reset time exists in [[Routine]], but a full workout is not assumed to fit inside a meal break. On training days, budget the real duration and explicitly adjust another flexible block. Choose the weekly frequency from actual capacity, rather than silently adding another hour daily.
