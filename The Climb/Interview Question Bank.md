## Readiness gate

**Unseen → attempted → review → ready**, with return to review when cold retrieval fails. A ready coding item needs a correct independent solution, reasoning, complexity and edge cases. A ready explanation/story needs coherent retrieval, truthful ownership and follow-up depth. No aggregate ATS/readiness percentage is generated.
