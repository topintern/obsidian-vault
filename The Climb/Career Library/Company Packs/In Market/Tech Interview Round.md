# Tech Interview Round

[[The Climb/Career Library/Career Library]]

> [!info] Source context
> Historical preparation or source notes. First-person stories and numeric outcomes remain source claims until confirmed; this import does not verify them.

This conversation will focus on understanding your hands-on experience with AI tools and how you approach building practical, impactful solutions. To help you prepare, here's what you can expect:

AI Tools Experience: Be ready to discuss the AI tools and technologies you've used (e.g., LLMs, AI-assisted IDEs, automation tools) and how they've helped you build or improve workflows.
Project Walkthroughs: You'll be asked to share specific examples of projects you've built using AI. What problem were you solving, what was your approach, and what were the results?
Execution & Systems Thinking: Expect questions that assess how you turn ambiguous problems into structured, working systems and your ability to move quickly from idea to execution.
Innovation & Collaboration: Be prepared to demonstrate how you think critically, experiment, and partner with others to deliver scalable solutions.
This is a great opportunity to showcase your creativity, technical skills, and ability to build real-world applications using AI. We encourage you to come ready with concrete examples and to speak openly about your thought process and learnings.
