## **AI Builder**

InMarket

Austin, Texas

*contract*

HYBRIDENTRY LEVEL

### Job Summary

The AI Builder designs and builds internal software utilizing AI to simplify, automate, and scale workflows across the organization. Responsibilities include owning the end-to-end lifecycle of internal tools, designing AI-enabled systems, modernizing technical debt, collaborating with various departments to identify friction points, managing stakeholders, and driving adoption through training materials. Required skills include expertise in AI-augmented development, modern tooling, data and systems architecture, and excellent communication and collaboration abilities. The ideal candidate is a pragmatic builder with experience in software engineering and a strong ability to convey technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

### Required Qualifications

- •Proven expertise using AI-native IDEs (e.g., Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code) and LLMs to accelerate development

- •Deep familiarity with RESTful APIs, Git, CI/CD pipelines, and Agile methodologies

- •Ability to translate exploratory work from JupyterLab or ad-hoc scripts into maintainable, production-ready software

- •Solid understanding of SQL/NoSQL databases, data modeling, and structured data management

- •Ability to design for cloud environments while maintaining a strong foundation in software engineering principles

- •Skill in evaluating where AI provides genuine leverage versus traditional software solutions

- •A 'progress over perfection' mindset, with the ability to identify patterns, evaluate trade-offs, and prioritize high-impact solutions

- •Comfortable navigating messy, real-world business processes and technical challenges in a fast-paced environment

- •Strong troubleshooting skills with the ability to reason through complex system failures

- •Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders clearly

- •Experience working in fast-moving teams with iterative feedback and flexibility

### Desired Qualifications

- •Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience

- •Experience building internal platforms or operational tooling

- •Experience in Node.js, with a track record of building internal tools, services, and robust automations

- •Experience in Python and SQL; comfortable building tools, services and automations

- •Familiarity with workflow orchestration, job scheduling, or data pipelines

- •Exposure to containerization (Docker) and cloud-native tooling

- •Prior experience working directly with operations or business teams

### Additional Requirements

- •For candidates in California, Colorado, New York City, Illinois, Minnesota, New Jersey, Vermont and Massachusetts the Targeted Base Salary Range for this role is $90,000 - $100,000
