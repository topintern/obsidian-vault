# Current Interview Round Details Info

> [!info] Source context
> Historical preparation or source notes. First-person stories and numeric outcomes remain source claims until confirmed; this import does not verify them.

This conversation will focus on culture, collaboration, and product thinking. To help you prepare, here's what you can expect:

Working Across Styles: Be ready to discuss how you've collaborated with people who think very differently — domain experts, designers, non-technical stakeholders — and what made those partnerships work or not work.

Navigating Disagreement: You'll be asked about moments where you pushed back, were overruled, had a peer conflict, or changed your mind because someone else was right. Wei is looking for self-awareness, not polish.

Showing Up When It's Hard: Expect questions about production failures, debugging marathons, deadline pressure, and moments where you stayed when it would have been easier to punt. Also: showing up for a person, not just a problem.

Genuine Curiosity: Be prepared to talk about how you stay current, what drew you to this space, and how you actually get curious about the people you're building for — not just the requirements.

Building for Real Users: You'll be asked about tools built for non-technical users, simplifying complex things, and how you think about the gap between what users say they want and what they actually do.

InMarket Product Knowledge: Know GeoLink, LCI, Activation, InSights, InMarKit. Wei owns GeoLink end-to-end. She shipped Async Avails (49→16 min campaign setup, $6.7M retained) and got GeoLink Reports SUS from 72.5 to 91.25. Speak her language: SUS scores, time-on-task, revenue retained, YOY comparisons.

This round is ~30 min with Wei Tang, Principal Product Designer, 5 years at InMarket. Come ready with specific stories from Samay, Epik, Zemoso/Strateos. Avoid vague answers — Wei measures everything, He will probe for specifics.
