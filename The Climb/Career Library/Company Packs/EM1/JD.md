Hi Neel,

I'm Gunnar, co-founder and CTO at EM1
,
and I reached out because I've been looking for engineers who take
ownership, work across the full stack, and have built applications on top
of LLMs. Your background building LLM-powered agents and RAG workflows for
compliance use cases at Samay Systems, combined with your full-stack
production experience at Cardinal Health using PostgreSQL, AWS, and
Terraform, maps closely to what we're doing.

We're building an AI-based platform for emergency management and public
safety, with a focus on helping agencies plan, train, respond, and stay
compliant. We have a handful of bespoke software products that help with
their specific workflows, and an agent that works across those tools to
draft plan updates, citing best practices from across the country and
lessons from After Action Reports (AARs).

A few reasons EM1 is getting attention already:

- We work with over 40 different customers across the country.
- Our product is built to keep humans in the loop for a very
human-centric industry. We ground the LLM directly in doctrine relevant to
our customers and provide sentence-level citations of where the response
came from.
- We're a small team that combines software engineers with experienced
emergency-management operators.

This role is perfect for someone who wants real ownership across
product, backend, and infrastructure, shipping quickly in Next.js,
TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AWS, Terraform, and LLM workflows.

What makes it especially interesting is the feedback loop: you'd be
building for practitioners in public safety where the software can
materially improve preparedness and response, and you'd help shape both the
product and engineering culture from the ground up.

Quick details about the role:

- Full stack engineer
- Salary range $150k-200k
- Fully remote with twice yearly offsites
- Full healthcare coverage
