**Behavioural Questions and stories**

How to prepare:

First search / google answers / info about the question. find info articles, posts etc. and interesting examples, relate them to your experience, pick one from memory and form a story.

Repeated questions, Group questions by pattern etc. a story may answer multiple questions, in that case group the questions to the same story,  at the end you will have n such stories you can repeatedly use which answer different groups of these questions.

- **What are you looking for in your next role?**

- **Tell me about a time when you had a conflict with a co-worker.**

- **What is the most challenging aspect of your current project?**

- **How do you tackle challenges? Name a difficult challenge you faced while working on a project, how you overcame it, and what you learned.**

- **Tell me about a time you had a disagreement with your manager.**

- **Talk about a project you are most passionate about, or one where you did your best work.**

- **What is something that you had to push for in your previous projects?**

- **What is the most constructive feedback you have received in your career?**

- **What is something you had to persevere at for multiple months?**

- **Tell me about a time you met a tight deadline.**

- **Time management has become a necessary factor in productivity. Give an example of a time-management skill you've learned and applied at work.**

- **What is the most challenging project in or out of school that you have worked on in the last 6 months.**
