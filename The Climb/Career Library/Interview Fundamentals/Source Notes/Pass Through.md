**PASSTHROUGH PREP**

**Company Overview:**

Passthrough is a company that provides **automated solutions for the** **investor onboarding process**, particularly focused on **private markets**. Their main offerings include **electronic subscription documents** and compliance processes such as **Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks.**

Passthrough's platform allows investment managers to streamline the onboarding of investors, significantly **reducing the time and effort required to process documentation**. This includes features like **real-time visibility into the status of fundraising efforts**, intelligent mapping of beneficial owners, and automatic screening against sanctions lists.

The platform also offers seamless **API ****integration with various CRMs, investor portals, and data rooms*******, making it easier for fund administrators to manage investor data and compliance requirements

Security and privacy are key priorities for Passthrough, with data encrypted at rest and in transit, **SOC 2 Type II compliance*******, and regular penetration testing to safeguard sensitive information.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Glossary****:**

A data room, also known as a virtual data room (VDR), is a **secure online repository used for storing and sharing confidential information**. It is commonly **used during financial transactions, such as mergers and acquisitions (M&A), fundraising, legal proceedings, and audits**.

**SOC 2 Type II compliance is a certification** that ensures a service organization manages customer data based on **five "trust service principles"—security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy****.**

 SOC** 2 Type I**: Assesses the design of security processes at a specific point in time.

 SOC** 2 Type II**: Evaluates the operational effectiveness of these processes over a period (usually 6-12 months), can be achieved through GAP analysis.
