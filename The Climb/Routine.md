# Routine

[[The Climb/THE WALL|The Wall]] · [[The Climb/Today|Today]] · [[The Climb/Routine|Routine]] · [[The Climb/WALL|Weekly review]]

> [!tip] A rhythm, not a prison
> Wake around **7–8 AM**. Protect **2 hours of japa/parayana/stotras**. Put weekday recruiter, vendor, networking and warm-lead work in business hours; career-page applications fit the afternoon. Today is adapted from this rhythm, never copied blindly.

## Full-focus weekday

Times below use the vault's configured **America/Chicago** time zone, not an assumption about where you live. Adjust outreach to the recipient's working hours.

| Window | Work | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00–7:30 | Wake, wash, prepare | 30 min |
| 7:30–9:30 | Japa / parayana / stotras | 2 h |
| 9:30–12:00 | Recruiters/vendors, replies, referrals, LinkedIn/networking; prepare and send 20–30 targeted DMs when feasible | 2.5 h Hunt |
| 12:00–12:45 | Lunch / reset | 45 min |
| 12:45–14:45 | Career pages, role checks, resume tailoring and verified applications | 2 h Hunt |
| 14:45–17:15 | DSA: 3–4 deliberate problems, mixing new attempts and due reviews | 2.5 h Prep |
| 17:15–18:00 | Dinner / movement / reset | 45 min |
| 18:00–19:00 | Experience deep dive + behavioral rehearsal | 1 h Prep |
| 19:00–20:30 | Core CS / backend / frontend / cloud / AI engineering | 1.5 h Prep |
| 20:30–21:30 | One creative artifact or a chosen own-build experiment | 1 h |
| 21:30–22:30 | DDIA reading + system-design application | 1 h Prep |
| 22:30–23:00 | Record evidence, prepare next queue, wind down | 30 min |

**Budget:** Spirit 2 h + Hunt 4.5 h + Prep 6 h + creative/build 1 h = **13.5 focused hours**. Other time is explicitly accounted for. This is an intensive full-availability day, not an assumption that no work or other commitments exist.

The requested 6–8 h prep range remains the ambition. An 8 h prep day needs 2 h released elsewhere; it is not silently added after bedtime. The 50–100 application ambition is not guaranteed by a 4.5 h block: measure actual throughput before increasing volume or sacrificing fit.

## Workday, interview day, or late start

1. Put confirmed work, interviews, appointments, meals, and stop time on the page first. Old shift patterns are not assumed current.
2. Preserve completed work and the next literal resume point.
3. Put live-person work in the remaining recipient business-hour windows. If the window has passed, prepare it for the next suitable one.
4. State the available minutes and select a feasible subset. Explicitly record what was deferred; don't pretend all daily targets still fit.
5. Spiritual practice remains the 2 h normal target. If it cannot fit, record the actual amount and the deliberate adjustment rather than quietly redefining success.
6. Never duplicate unfinished tasks as catch-up debt. An urgent interview may replace general prep.

## Weekend shape

Spiritual practice and a study block remain. Use quieter hours for career pages, resume work, longer mocks, weekly review, movement, and social/creative work. Time-sensitive replies still matter, but scheduled cold outreach should respect recipients' working hours.

## Consistency

Log actual minutes, real submissions, sent outreach, and practice outcomes in the dated day record. Unknown is blank, not zero. The daily practice is recurring; an unchecked box from yesterday is not today's obligation.
